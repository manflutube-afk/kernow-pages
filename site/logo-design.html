Logo design for Cornish businesses.

A logo has to work everywhere you do: on the sign above the door, on a van, on a cap, and as a tiny circle on Instagram. I design ones that hold up in all of those places, for cafés, bands, trades and anyone else who needs one.

Word of Mouth

Band

The guitar body becomes the O of “Word” and the O of “Mouth”, with THE running up the neck. Stack the W on its mirror image and you get a monogram that’s small enough for a plectrum, a guitar strap or the front of a cap.

Heal

Voice actor · two logos, one name

A voice actor who wanted two logos: one for social media, and one for his equipment, clothing and merch. Both spell out HEAL, and in both the H is where the meaning is.

On the kit and the merch, the H is a sound wave. Its two tallest peaks are the uprights, and the wave runs on into a handwritten “eal”. It’s one colour and one line, so it can be embroidered on a cap or etched onto a pop filter and still read clearly.

On social media, the H is a stick man holding a microphone up to his mouth: the voice behind the voice work. Framing it in a strip of film keeps it loose and personal, a face for the account rather than a label for the kit.

Questions people ask

How much does a logo cost?

It depends on what the logo needs to do. A simple wordmark is a different job from a full set with a monogram and a pattern. Fill in the brief and I’ll give you a price before any work starts. Asking costs nothing.

I’ve no idea what I want. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Most people don’t know until they see something. The brief’s questions are there to help: what you do, who your customers are, and the feel you’re after. Working out the rest is my job.

Can you tidy up a logo I already have?

Yes. Choose “A refresh of the one I’ve got” in the brief and tell me what’s not working about it now. Often it just needs redrawing so it prints cleanly.

Can you build the website as well?

Yes. That’s most of what I do. A logo and a website designed together look like they belong together. Say so in the brief, or see the website prices.

Ready when you are.

The brief takes about five minutes. Answer what you can and leave the rest to me.
